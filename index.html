Explore My Journey

This website exists to show what my life is really about. I was a squash player who loved the sport but was not good enough to go professional. When my eligibility ended, I decided to move on to bigger challenges. I wanted to prove to myself that I could accomplish something physically demanding that not many people ever do, which is why I am now training toward Ironman.

Along the way, I build startups, explore new ideas, and try to meet as many people as possible. Whether through sport, projects, or simple conversations, my goal is always the same. Create impact, grow through challenge, and leave people feeling better than before.

About

About Me

I am originally from the Czech Republic. Growing up, most of my identity and social life revolved around squash, which eventually pushed me to seek a fresh start beyond what I knew. When I was twenty, I moved to the United States to play college squash and begin a completely new chapter of my life.

I spent my first two years at Franklin and Marshall College in Lancaster, Pennsylvania. The team was special, and some of my closest friendships still come from that group. At the same time, it was a small school, and socially it felt limiting. Outside of your immediate circle, it was hard to meet new people. Despite being number one on the team and competing at a high level, squash existed inside a small bubble that few people outside it cared about.

Athlete

Growth lives
beyond comfort

I first stepped onto a squash court when I was seven years old. Over time, my playing style evolved from technical to endurance focused. I became the grinder who stayed on court longer and trusted consistency over flair.

Around 2020, physical testing revealed strong endurance potential, which introduced me to Ironman training. Endurance appealed to me because it is simple and honest. You show up, put in the time, and progress follows.

My squash career included competing at the World Junior Championships, traveling internationally, and reaching a highest world ranking of 202. Those experiences shaped how I approach effort, discipline, and long-term commitment.

Philosophy

Be the energy you seek

How you show up matters more than what you say. People remember how they felt around you. I believe growth lives beyond comfort and that impact often starts small, through conversation and presence.

My goal is simple. Keep showing up, keep learning, and leave people feeling better than before.
